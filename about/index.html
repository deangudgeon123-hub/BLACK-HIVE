About Stone & Anvil

Stone & Anvil began with a simple idea: make beautiful jewellery with a lighter footprint. We create handcrafted pieces in recycled silver, taking time with each form and finish.

Our process

From first sketch to final polish, every piece is made by hand. Gentle hammer textures, considered proportions, and careful finishing give each design a quiet individuality.

Sustainability

We prioritise recycled silver and small-batch making to reduce excess and honour the materials we use. Our goal is jewellery made to last, wear well, and be treasured for years.
